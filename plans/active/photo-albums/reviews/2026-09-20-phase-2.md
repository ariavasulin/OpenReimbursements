## Removal-related finding

### 1. Medium — shortened forms retain a large, purposeless empty middle

**Evidence:** [Upload, desktop 1440×1000](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/upload-desktop-1440.png), [upload, phone 390×844](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/upload-phone-390.png), [edit, desktop 1440×1000](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/edit-desktop-1440.png), [edit, phone 390×844](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/edit-phone-390.png).

The upload form correctly presents preview → Job → Tags, but approximately 320px of blank space separates the last desktop field from the footer; the phone gap is approximately 360px. The edit form has only Tags and the move-review section, followed by roughly 270–310px of blank space before Save. These are settled captures, including a fresh phone edit opening rather than an in-transition resize.

**Why it matters:** the forms look as though content failed to load or a field was removed without resizing its container. An older, non-technical user has to visually reconnect a short form at the top with its action far below. This violates content-driven density and proximity; the empty area is substantially larger than the useful form.

**Suggested fix:** let both dialogs size to their remaining content, with 24–32px between the final content group and footer and a viewport-relative maximum height for overflow. Preserve scrolling for tags and larger selections. The edit divider correctly separates moving from tag editing; it does not need removal.

**Attribution:** directly relevant to the post-removal result, but not a proven newly introduced regression. No before-build or source comparison was authorized, so I cannot establish how much of the oversized container already existed. The removal leaves this imbalance visible now.

## Other observed issues — not attributed to the change set

### 2. Medium — phone forms have no visible Cancel or Close control

**Evidence:** [Upload, phone 390×844](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/upload-phone-390.png) and [edit, phone 390×844](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/edit-phone-390.png).

Both phone drawers show a drag handle and a primary Upload/Save button, but no labeled cancellation action or close icon. Their desktop equivalents do show an ×. For someone unsure about uploading the selected file or changing a photo, the only explicit button appears to commit the operation. A swipe or outside tap is an undisclosed exit, particularly for the older audience.

**Suggested fix:** retain the handle but add a visible, keyboard-reachable Close button or a Cancel action beside the primary action, with a 44px touch target. This is an existing drawer-pattern concern outside the described removals; its presence in a previous build was not independently verified.

### 3. Low — restore review uses technical language before showing the selected photo

**Evidence:** [Restore review, desktop 1440×1000](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/restore-desktop-1440.png), [restore review, phone 390×844](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/restore-phone-390.png), and [trash review, phone 390×844](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/trash-review-phone-390.png).

“Canonical photo,” “exact target,” “current owning job,” and “concurrent changes” describe implementation rather than the user's task. On the phone restore screen this copy and the draft/action panel push the selected item's row to the bottom edge of the initial viewport, below Confirm restore. The trash entry screen also says to review a photo “below” before any photo is shown.

**Why it matters:** people trying to recover a photo need to recognize the item and destination before deciding; the explanatory text makes a small, familiar action feel like an administrative procedure.

**Suggested fix:** put the selected file and job above the restore action, say “Restore 1 photo to [job],” and disclose duplicate/conflict detail only when relevant. Keep the 30-day recovery explanation visible. This is a broader review-flow concern, not evidence that widening action availability caused a new defect.

## Coverage and limits

Every requested surface was visually inspected at **390×844 and 1440×1000**, in the existing dark theme. Additional project-page reflow checks at [320×900](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/project-extra-320.png), [375×900](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/project-extra-375.png), and [768×900](../../../../.artifacts/photo-albums/reviews/2026-09-20-phase-2/project-extra-768.png) showed the filters and content fitting; Rename wraps to its own line at 320px without collision. A 200% text-resize pass and a full keyboard/accessibility audit were not completed, so this is not an accessibility certification.

The visible project had 40 items, all under the same uploader; its Uploader menu offered only that person. I therefore could not verify the newly broadened trash action on another person's photo. Trash had one recoverable `.xmp` item with “No preview”; its row and restore review were reachable, but a populated image thumbnail and multiple-owner trash list were unavailable. I did not create data to fill those gaps. There is no before-build evidence establishing a defect as newly caused or conclusively pre-existing; the report separates removal-related imbalance from unrelated observations instead of inventing that certainty.

**Recommended next action:** tighten the two dialog heights first. Treat the cancellation affordance and review copy as separate follow-ups. The smallest useful feedback is whether the shortened forms may use content-driven height; no broader redesign is needed.

**Verdict:** the removal itself looks finished in the library, search, and viewer: no Sheet remnants, gaps in metadata, or awkward project-header controls remain in the inspected states. The upload and edit dialogs do not yet look finished because their blank lower halves overwhelm the remaining content. Trash rows and action placement are clean at both requested sizes, while restore review has older-looking copy and hierarchy issues that cannot be blamed on this change set. Cross-owner action availability and image-bearing trash rows remain unverified because the available data did not expose them.
